Dmitri Mendeleev

Architect of the Periodic Table & Father of Modern Chemistry

Biography

Born in Siberia in 1834, Dmitri Mendeleev transformed chemistry from a loose collection of facts into a precise, predictive science. His creation of the periodic system shaped modern scientific discovery forever.

The Periodic Law

In 1869, he organized elements by their atomic weight. He left empty slots for undiscovered elements and correctly predicted their exact properties years before they were actually found.
