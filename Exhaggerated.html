Biography

Born in Tobolsk, Siberia, in 1834, Dmitri Ivanovich Mendeleev was a visionary Russian chemist and inventor. He transformed chemistry from a loose collection of facts into a precise, predictive science. His relentless curiosity and dedication to education shaped the scientific world forever.

Key Scientific Breakthroughs

The Periodic Law

In 1869, he organized elements by their atomic weight, discovering that chemical properties repeat periodically.

Flawless Predictions

Mendeleev left empty slots in his table for undiscovered elements (like Gallium and Germanium) and correctly predicted their properties years before they were found.

Chemical Nomenclature

He standardized how elements were classified, creating a universal language for scientists around the globe.

Honoured in the Stars: Element 101

In 1955, American scientists discovered a new synthetic element. They named it after Mendeleev to acknowledge his immense impact on chemical organization.
